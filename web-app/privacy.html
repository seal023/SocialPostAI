Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

SocialPostAI Pro ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI-powered social media copy generation service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Topics you submit. The text prompts and selected platforms you enter are sent to our AI provider (Alibaba Cloud Bailian) to generate copy. Prompts are processed to fulfill your request and are not used to train public models.
  • Waitlist emails. If you join the waitlist, we store your email address solely to notify you when payments and Pro features go live.
  • Usage data. We store anonymous, aggregated usage counters (e.g., daily generation counts) to enforce free-tier limits. These are not linked to your identity.

2. How we use information

We use submitted topics only to generate your requested copy. We use waitlist emails only for launch notifications. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. AI and third-party processors

Generation requests are processed by Alibaba Cloud Bailian (DashScope) acting as our data-processing subcontractor. Your prompts are transmitted over HTTPS and used solely to produce your copy.

4. Data retention

Waitlist emails are retained until you request deletion or until the Service is discontinued. Generated copy is not stored on our servers beyond the duration needed to return it to your browser.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your waitlist email at any time by contacting support@socialpostai.app.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
