Last updated: November 10, 2026

All payments for SocialPostAI Pro are processed by our payment partner, Creem. This policy describes when refunds may be granted.

Credit packs are non-subscription, pay-as-you-go purchases. Refunds are considered within 14 days if the pack has not been substantially used (less than 10% consumed).

Email support@socialpostai.app with your purchase email and order reference. We will review and respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Creem.
